1-9/2025(SA-III) I/16012/2025 FAQs for ISHAN UDAY SCHOLARSHIP

### 1. Can all NER students apply for the scholarship?

Reply: No, the students with North Eastern domicile are eligible to apply. The income of the parents of the student should be below Rs. 04.50 lakh per annum.

### 2. Are students required to have a domicile certificate of NER ?

Reply: Yes, student should have a valid Domicile Certificate of any State of NER.

3. Can Student apply in the UG Scholarship when he has enrolled in Integrated programme.

Reply: In case of integrated programmes, the scholarship shall be given for UG component only.

4. How many students are selected under Ishan Uday scheme.

Reply: Ten thousand (10000) per annum.

### 5. What is the amount of the scholarship?

Reply: The scholarship is given at the rate of Rs. 8,000/-per month for 10 months in a year.

6. Those students who have completed UG degree and want to do UG again, can they still

apply?

Reply: Students, who have already acquired any UG degree, are not eligible to apply. The scholarship is available for 1st UG degree only.

### 7. Can students who are doing UG through distance mode apply for Ishan Uday Scheme?

Reply: No, this scholarship is for regular mode students only.

### 8. If a student who gets another scholarship can apply for this scheme ?

Reply: The students can avail only one scholarship at a time.

9. What is the procedure of selection under this scholarship.

Reply: 50% of slots are earmarked for Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, Law, Management and other 50% are earmarked for Science, Engineering & Technology, medical, technical, agriculture, forestry programmes. The selection is done purely on the basis of state-wise merit from among the candidates who have applied for the scholarship under the scheme.

### 10. How many times applications are invited in a year?

Reply: Applications are invited once in a year on the National Scholarship Portal

(NSP) of the Government of India.

### 11. I have submitted the application. Am I eligible to receive the scholarship?

12. What is the procedure of scholarship disbursement.

Reply: The scholarship amount is disbursed by UGC through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) Mode and transferred to the Bank account which is seeded with Aadhaar.

### 13. Can the application be canceled after verification by the institute?

Reply: The application may be canceled if the student or institution provides wrong/misleading information on the portal.

### 14. Is it necessary to have AISHE code for university or college?

Reply: Yes, it is mandatory for the University/College/Institute to have a valid AISHE code for accessing the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The institution with AISHE code can get themselves registered on NSP portal through District/State nodal officers for NSP.

### 15. How to know the deadlines for application submission and verification of application at Institute/District/State Level?

Reply: The deadlines for application submission and verification are displayed on NSP.

### 16. How to know the contact details of various Nodal Officers?

Reply: The Scheme wise details of Nodal Officers is available under the Public Link of the NSP Portal.

17. Which students can apply for renewal in NSPG .

Reply: The scholarship are renewed for the next year of study on successful completion of first year of study. The students are required to apply for ‘Renewal’ of their scholarship on the NSP. Scholarship for sub-sequent years will not be processed without successful submission of renewal application.

### 18. Can students edit the application after closing date?

Reply: No, student cannot edit the application after closing date. Incomplete applications will be summarily rejected. Only verified online applications will be considered for award of scholarship.
