Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students

### 1. What is the objective of the Scheme?

Answer: The Scheme aims to promote quality education among Scheduled Caste (SC) students by providing financial support for pursuing studies beyond Class XII in notified institutions.

### 2. Who is eligible?

Answer: SC students with annual family income up to Rs.8.00 lakh, admitted in a full-time prescribed course in a notified institution, subject to the allotted slots.

### 3. Who can apply for Fresh Scholarship?

Answer: Only first-year students admitted during the current academic year are eligible for Fresh Scholarship.

### 4. Can second/third/fourth year students apply as Fresh?

Answer: No. Fresh Scholarship is available only to first-year students.

### 5. Which institutions are covered?

Answer: The Scheme covers notified institutions including IITs, NITs, IIITs, IIMs, AIIMS, NLUs, IHMs, NIFTs, NIDs, Central Government Institutions and other notified institutions empanelled by the Ministry.

### 6. How are scholarships allotted?

Answer: Scholarships are awarded strictly on inter-se merit based on the admission criteria prescribed for the course, subject to the number of slots allotted to the institution.

### 7. What if eligible students exceed available slots?

Answer: Scholarship is restricted to students within the allotted slots. In case of a tie for the last slot, preference is given to the student having lower

### 8. Is there reservation for girl students?

Answer: Yes. Thirty percent of allotted slots are reserved for eligible SC girl students. Unutilised reserved slots may be transferred to eligible boys as per merit.

### 9. What documents are required?

Answer: Valid SC Certificate, Income Certificate, Aadhaar, Aadhaar-seeded bank account details, admission details, admission rank and fee details as prescribed on NSP.

### 10. What are the income certificate requirements?

Answer: Self-employed parents: certificate by Revenue Officer not below Tehsildar. Salaried parents: employer certificate and Revenue Officer certificate for other income.

### 11. What financial assistance is provided?

Answer: Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges (up to Rs.2 lakh per annum in private institutions), Academic Allowance of Rs.86,000 in first year and Rs.41,000 per year thereafter.

### 12. How is scholarship released?

Answer: Scholarship is released through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the student.

### 13. Is Aadhaar seeding mandatory?

Answer: Yes. Aadhaar-seeded bank account is mandatory for DBT.

### 14. How can students apply?

Answer: Students must submit applications online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

### 15. What is the role of the institute?

Answer: Institutes verify applications, ensure slot limits, verify documents and forward eligible applications on NSP within prescribed timelines.

### 16. What is the last date for institute verification?

Answer: 31 October every year or any other date notified by DBT Mission.

### 17. Can more than two siblings receive scholarship?

Answer: No. Benefits are restricted to two siblings. An affidavit is required.

### 18. Can a student receive another scholarship?

Answer: No. A beneficiary cannot simultaneously avail similar scholarship from Central/State Government.

### 19. What happens if a student fails?

Answer: Scholarship is withheld until promotion. Once promoted, scholarship is restored automatically.

### 20. What happens if a student changes institute?

Answer: Scholarship under this Scheme is discontinued.

### 21. What happens if incorrect information is furnished?

Answer: Scholarship may be cancelled and recovery/action may be initiated as per rules.

### 22. Can an institute be removed from the Scheme?

Answer: Yes. Institutions may be de-notified for violation of Scheme provisions, non-participation for three consecutive years, absence of AISHE code or NSP KYC, or other reasons as per guidelines.

### 23. Will existing beneficiaries continue if an institute is de-notified?

Answer: Yes. Existing eligible beneficiaries continue till completion of the course. No fresh slots are allotted thereafter.

### 24. Where can students seek help?

Answer: Students should first contact the Institute Nodal Officer. NSP- related technical issues may also be taken up through NSP Helpdesk.

### 25. Where are Scheme Guidelines available?

Answer: Latest Scheme Guidelines are available on the Department's website and National Scholarship Portal (NSP).
